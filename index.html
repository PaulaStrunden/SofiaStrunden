About

Sofia Strunden is a graphic designer, creative concept developer and crossover-media instructor based in Köln.

Her practice moves between print, illustration, 3D and interactive media — from analogue Risograph and clay to Blender, Cinema4D, Unity and motion capture. She is interested in the body as material: how interior states, breath and attention can be drawn, scanned, modelled and played.

Recent work ranges from the interactive installation and Bachelor thesis koerpern (Folkwang Universität der Künste, 2021) to the 3D mobile game Perle 3000 (Filmakademie Baden-Württemberg, 2024 – 2025).

Alongside her own projects she teaches and mentors at Tinkertank and STORE Projects London, working with young people in the space between craft, electronics, code and design — thinking with the hands, and letting ideas grow from mistakes.
