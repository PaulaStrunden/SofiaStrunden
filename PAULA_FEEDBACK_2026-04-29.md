## Rework About text

> Sofia Strunden is a graphic designer, creative concept developer and crossover-media instructor based in Köln.
>
> Her practice moves between print, illustration, 3D and interactive media — from analogue printing techniques and bookbinding to digital 3D environments for game design and animation. She is interested in how interior states, attention can be drawn, scanned, modelled and played.
>
> Recent work ranges from the interactive installation and Bachelor thesis koerpern (Folkwang Universität der Künste, 2021) to the 3D mobile game Perle 3000 (Filmakademie Baden-Württemberg, 2024 – 2025).
>
> Alongside her own projects she teaches and mentors at Tinkertank and STORE Projects London, working with young people in the space between craft, electronics, code and design — thinking with the hands, and letting ideas grow from collaborative learning.
